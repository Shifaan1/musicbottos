Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 20, 2026
🔒 Data Protection Notice

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information our experimental Music Bot (the "Bot") processes when you add it to your Discord server or interact with it.

1. Information the Bot Collects & Processes

To perform its core functions as a music player, the Bot processes standard Discord data, which may include:

  • Server Identifiers: Server IDs, channel IDs, and voice channel IDs to connect and stream audio.
  • User Interactions: User IDs and text commands sent to the Bot to register playback requests (e.g., play, skip, queue).
  • Audio Metadata: Temporary track names and URLs needed to fetch and queue music.

2. How Data is Used

Any data accessed by the Bot is used exclusively to provide functional music playback features within your active Discord session. We do not use this data for marketing, profiling, or tracking purposes.

3. Data Storage & Retention

  • No Audio Recording: The Bot streams music into voice channels but NEVER records, saves, or monitors your voice chats or background audio.
  • Temporary Caching: Active music queues and command data are stored temporarily in volatile memory (RAM) and are completely cleared when the Bot leaves a voice channel, restarts, or crashes.
  • Persistent Storage: During this experimental phase, we do not maintain a permanent database of user behavior, history, or settings.

4. Data Sharing & Third-Party Services

We do not sell, trade, or share your information with outside parties. However, the Bot interacts with the official Discord API to operate. When fetching music tracks, requests are sent to public audio hosting platforms (like YouTube or SoundCloud metadata endpoints) solely to stream the requested song file.

5. User Rights & Data Removal

Because we do not store long-term personal data in a database, there is no permanent tracking data to delete. If you wish to stop all real-time data processing, simply remove (kick or ban) the Bot from your Discord server.

6. Revisions to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the Bot develops and new features are tested. We encourage users to check this page occasionally to stay informed.

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or the Bot's behavior, please reach out to the project developer via Discord.
